# Compliance

## Ethics & Compliance Policy

### What The System Monitors

- Public posts and threads
- Public forums and discussion boards
- Public event listings
- Public social media profiles and pages

### What The System Must NOT Do

- Scrape private WhatsApp groups or chats
- Access private Facebook groups without permission
- Collect sensitive personal data without lawful basis
- Send unsolicited bulk messages
- Store data beyond what is necessary for the business purpose

### Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 Compliance

This system processes personal data and must comply with Kenya's Data Protection Act (DPA) 2019, administered by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

Key obligations:

- **Lawful basis**: Ensure a lawful basis exists for processing any personal data collected (legitimate interest applies to publicly posted data in most cases).
- **Data minimisation**: Only collect what is necessary to assess and respond to a transport inquiry.
- **Retention limits**: Do not retain personal data from leads that did not convert beyond 90 days unless there is a clear business reason.
- **Security**: Store lead data securely. Restrict access to the business owner and any authorised staff only.
- **Registration**: If processing personal data at scale, register with the ODPC (odpc.go.ke).
- **No re-identification**: Do not attempt to identify anonymous posts by cross-referencing other data sources.

### WhatsApp Business Compliance

- Use approved message templates for initial outreach
- Respect opt-out requests immediately
- Maximum 2 follow-up attempts per unresponsive lead
- No broadcast messaging to non-opt-in contacts

### Data Retention Schedule

| Data Type | Retention Period | Action After Expiry |
|-----------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Converted lead records | 2 years | Archive |
| Non-converted lead data | 90 days | Anonymise or delete |
| Flight delay alerts | 7 days | Delete |
| Seasonal monitoring notes | Ongoing | Keep as operational metadata (no PII) |
