# Partnership Strategy

## High-Value Partners

- Boutique hotels and serviced apartments
- Airbnb hosts with frequent international guests
- Conference and event organisers
- NGOs and international development organisations
- Safari and tour operators (airport–lodge transfers)
- Wedding planners
- Embassies and high commissions (delegation transport)

## Partnership Offer

Provide:
- Reliable airport pickups and drop-offs
- Conference group shuttles
- Late-night and early-morning airport runs
- Guest transport support for hotels without a shuttle
- Recurring NGO transport arrangements

## Competitive Positioning

Position as:
- Punctual and dependable
- Safe and professional
- Airport specialists (JKIA and Wilson)
- Available for late-night and irregular hours
- Flexible for groups of varying sizes

## Recurring Revenue Opportunities

- Hotel transport contracts (per-guest or monthly retainer)
- NGO transport retainers (monthly or per-project)
- Conference logistics agreements
- Safari operator airport transfer partnerships
