# Seasonal Calendar Awareness

Apply seasonal boosters to scoring and alerts during known high-demand periods.

## High-Demand Periods in Nairobi

| Period | Reason | Scoring Boost |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| January–March | Donor review missions, Q1 NGO field visits | NGO leads +10 |
| April | Easter travel peak | Airport transfer leads +10 |
| June–July | Mid-year NGO conferences, African Union sessions | Conference + NGO leads +15 |
| August | UN General Assembly preparation, safari peak season | All categories +10 |
| September–October | UNGA travel, international conference peak | Airport + Conference leads +20 |
| November | End-of-year NGO missions and reporting | NGO leads +10 |
| December | Holiday travel peak, Christmas and New Year | Airport transfer leads +20 |

## Key Annual Events to Pre-Monitor

Pre-load these dates at start of each year. Begin scraping delegate travel signals 3–4 weeks before each event.

- African Union Summit (usually January/February — Addis, but delegates transit Nairobi)
- UNCTAD / UNEP sessions — Gigiri, Nairobi
- Africa Climate Week (varies — often Nairobi)
- Kenya Tourism Expo (Nairobi, annual)
- East Africa Business Summit
- Nairobi Innovation Week
- United Nations General Assembly (September — UN HQ in NY, but Nairobi office coordinates)
- East Africa Standover Forum
- World Bank/IMF Spring & Annual Meetings (delegates transit through Nairobi)

## Implementation

When running the daily workflow, check the current date against this calendar. If a high-demand period is active or approaching:

1. Add the relevant scoring boost to all leads in affected categories
2. Increase monitoring frequency for related source types (e.g., more NGO platform checks in Q1)
3. Note seasonal context in the morning digest under the SEASONAL NOTE section
