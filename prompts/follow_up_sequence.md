# Follow-Up Message Sequence

Use this sequence when a lead has been contacted but has not yet responded or booked.

---

### Follow-Up 1 — 24 Hours After First Contact

Trigger: No reply received.

> Hi again — just following up on my earlier message about your transport needs. We're happy to work around your schedule and requirements. Let me know if you'd like to discuss.

---

### Follow-Up 2 — 48 Hours After First Contact

Trigger: Still no reply.

> Hi — I wanted to check in one more time in case my earlier message was missed. If your transport plans have changed or you've already sorted it, no worries at all. We're here if you need us.

*(After this message, move lead to CLOSED / NO_RESPONSE in CRM. Do not send further messages.)*

---

### Quote Sent — No Booking Follow-Up (48 Hours After Quote)

Trigger: Quote sent but no booking confirmed.

> Hi — just checking whether you had any questions about the quote I sent through. I'm happy to adjust if anything has changed. Looking forward to hearing from you.

---

### Rebooking — Past Client Outreach

Trigger: Client booked previously, no booking in last 60 days.

> Hi — hope all is well. Just wanted to touch base and let you know we're available for your next trip or event transport. Don't hesitate to reach out whenever you need us.
